Move with arrow keys or WASD. Tap Space to slash, hold it to charge a spin attack, and press Shift to dodge roll.
Read the red ink. Every monster warns you before it strikes: a landing circle, a dive line, a sword cone, a charge arrow. Roll out of the way.
Fight, then answer. Knock a monster down and its question appears. Miss it and the monster gets back up. Don't spam guesses: a wrong answer clicked too fast is a rushed guess. It costs 2 hearts, wipes your streak, and fully heals the monster.
Fell in battle? A Spirit Gate at the entrance offers one very hard question. Get it right and you go straight back to where you fell.
You can always run. While a monster lives, the doors ahead stay sealed, but the way you came in is always open.
The scarier the monster, the harder its question. Watch their colors.
Build a streak. Answer right on the first try, with no help, and your hero powers up: sharper, faster, fearless, legendary. A miss knocks you down one level.
Earn XP and trade it with hidden merchants for armor and blades. Open chests for potions, scrolls, feathers, tonics, and help fairies.
Bosses lurk in lairs. The Lich hides behind a magic seal, and it takes two boss trophies to break it. Each lair also guards legendary treasure.
Look closely. Not every wall is solid. Somewhere there's a map.
