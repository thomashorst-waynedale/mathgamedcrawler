Class code: share this with students

Students type the code on the title screen and tap Use code, and their topic checkboxes fill in to match. Nothing is locked: they can still change things after. The code never expires and works offline. The same choices always make the same code, so you can reuse it all year.

The Algebra Keep

Somewhere in this keep, the Variable Lich waits on its throne. Each part of the keep narrows to a boss lair that guards the way on. Sometimes you get to choose which boss to face. Knock monsters down with your sword, then answer their questions to finish them off. A run takes about 15 minutes.

Tap how far the class has gotten, or check topics one by one. Tap "skills" next to a topic to pick only some parts of it. Monsters, bosses, and the Lich only ask about checked topics. Difficulty still rises the deeper you go.

Combat difficulty

This only changes the sword fighting. The math questions stay the same. You can switch anytime from the Menu.

Move with arrow keys or WASD. Tap Space to slash (every third slash in a row is a stronger finisher, then your sword needs a moment), hold it to charge a spin attack. Mashing only slows your sword down, and press Shift to dodge roll.
Read the red ink. Every monster warns you before it strikes: a landing circle, a dive line, a sword cone, a charge arrow. Roll out of the way.
Fight, then answer. Knock a monster down and its question appears. Miss it and the monster gets back up. Don't spam guesses: a wrong answer clicked too fast is a rushed guess. It costs 2 hearts, wipes your streak, and fully heals the monster.
Mistakes come back. A question you miss comes back later, in a room you've already cleared, as a purple Vengeful monster. It waits behind a shield while two Echoes come at you one at a time with easier versions of the same kind of problem. Beat both, then beat the one you missed.
Falling costs you. Your streak drops one level and you drop half your unspent XP where you fell. Beating a lair boss lights a waystone there: that becomes your checkpoint, with its own Training Dummy for rebuilding your streak. Bosses remember the steps you already beat. Walk back to grab it before you fall again, or it's gone. A Spirit Gate at the entrance offers one very hard question to send you straight back.
You can always run. While a monster lives, the doors ahead stay sealed, but the way you came in is always open.
The scarier the monster, the harder its question. Watch their colors.
Build a streak. Answer right on the first try, with no help, and your hero powers up: sharper, faster, fearless, legendary. A miss knocks you down one level.
Earn XP and trade it with hidden merchants for armor and blades. Open chests for potions, scrolls, feathers, tonics, and help fairies.
Four biomes. Each adventure lands in the Keep, the Frozen Caverns (slippery ice), the Sunken Ruins (deep water slows you) or the Ember Forge (lava cracks flare). Each has its own monsters, bosses and tricks.
Beat the final boss to go deeper. A staircase opens to a second, deeper dungeon in a new biome. You keep your gear, XP and items. Down there every monster asks a two-part problem, many mixing two topics.
Bosses lurk in lairs. The Lich hides behind a magic seal, and it takes two boss trophies to break it. Each lair also guards legendary treasure.
Look closely. Not every wall is solid. Somewhere there's a map.
